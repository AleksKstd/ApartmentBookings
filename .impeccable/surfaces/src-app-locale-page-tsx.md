# Public site: homepage (and the public pages that share its world)

Scope: `src/app/[locale]/` (home first; apartment, gallery, booking, confirmation, contact follow the same world). Visitor mode: Persuade on home/apartment/gallery/contact; Operate on booking and confirmation.

Audience and job: Bulgarian and foreign visitors coming to Burgas for concerts, festivals or the beach, picking exact dates. Action: search dates → see the full price → send a booking request. Proof: the location (in a house beside Bogoridi Boulevard), the honest total, booking direct with no fees. Constraints: PRODUCT.md; no invented reviews, events or walking times.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a street corner in central Burgas. Its parts are cobalt enamel plates on a lime-washed wall; it refuses the holiday-rental default of a photo carousel with a headline and a button over it.

OWN-WORLD: Plaster-white ground; cobalt enamel plates with a white inset rule and small rounded corners carry every action, label and sign; dark cobalt for the deepest surfaces; Sofia Sans Condensed (heavy) for headings and plate lettering, Sofia Sans for reading text; Cyrillic first on signs.

STORY: The visitor sees where the apartment is (a real street plaque, Bogoridi Blvd), puts their dates into the search plate, and reaches an honest price; the signpost of walking times proves the location.
